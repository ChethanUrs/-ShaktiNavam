Explore Mysuru

"Timeless Beauty in the Cultural Capital of Karnataka"

A Legacy of Grandeur

Mysuru, often called the Cultural Capital of Karnataka, is a city rich in royal heritage, spirituality, and vibrant traditions. Known worldwide for the grand Dasara festival, Mysuru beautifully reflects the legacy of the Wadiyar dynasty through its magnificent palaces, temples, and historic architecture.

The city is famous for Mysore Silk, sandalwood products, yoga heritage, and its calm, well-planned streets. With a perfect blend of culture, history, and modern charm, Mysuru offers visitors a peaceful yet culturally enriching experience.

Nearby Attractions

Srirangapatna

Located about 15 km away, this historic town on the banks of the River Kaveri was once the capital of Tipu Sultan. Famous for the Ranganathaswamy Temple and historic forts.

Chamundi Hills

Around 13 km from the city, these sacred hills offer panoramic views of Mysuru and house the famous Chamundeshwari Temple.

Krishna Raja Sagar (KRS) Dam

About 20 km away, popular for the beautiful Brindavan Gardens and mesmerizing evening musical fountain shows.

Bandipur National Park

Around 80 km from Mysuru, a haven for nature lovers known for its wildlife safaris and breathtaking forest landscapes.

Talakadu

About 45 km away, unique and famous for its ancient temples buried under mysterious sand dunes.
